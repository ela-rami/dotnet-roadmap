# Backend .NET — from scratch to a strong junior

This is my personal course for becoming a **backend C#/.NET developer** who can handle junior interviews (and roles asking for 1–2 years of experience) at international companies — ideally in Vienna, or fully remote.

It's built **one lesson at a time**: there is a clear goal, but the next lesson is chosen after seeing how the previous one went.
